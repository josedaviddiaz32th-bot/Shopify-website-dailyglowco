## Notes for building ads from this library

- The only images safe to use with no caveat today: the in-use shots (as demonstrations) and real product photos/renders (e.g. the hero shots on the store).
- Match the patch to the product in the ad: crescent eye pieces belong to the Face Set; the Eye Patches product is T-shaped (use the store hero `wrinkle-smooth-eye-patches-hero.webp`).
- Backgrounds in these shots are light grey studio; tanks are grey or deep teal (#17393D), which sit well on the mint #F4F8F6 ad canvas.
